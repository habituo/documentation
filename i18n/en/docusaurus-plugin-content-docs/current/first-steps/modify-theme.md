# Style Customization

At **Habituo**, we believe that every user should feel comfortable with their environment, which is why you can **completely customize the platform's appearance** according to your taste or mood.

Our goal is for Habituo not only to help you improve your habits but also to be visually pleasant and motivating.

---

## ⚙️ What can you customize?

From the **customization panel**, you can adjust the following design aspects:

- **Typography:** choose the font you like best for all the text across the platform.
- **Primary Color:** select the color that will represent your general theme (buttons, accents, and details).  
- **Borders (border-radius):** adjust the roundness of elements: from sharp corners to fully rounded edges.
- **Light/Dark Mode:** switch between a bright style or a dark mode to protect your eyes or adapt to your surroundings.

Every change will be applied **in real-time**, so you can see the result instantly.

## 💾 Automatic Saving

Your settings are saved automatically, so **you won't lose your personalized style** even if you log out or switch devices.
You can always change it again whenever you want, with complete freedom.

---

## 🌈 Make it Yours

Whether you prefer a minimalist, colorful design, with soft or sharp edges, **Habituo adapts to you**.
Your space for productivity and personal improvement can also reflect your style and personality.
