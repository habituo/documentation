# Create an Account and Log In

Before you start creating and managing your habits in **Habituo**, you need to have an account. This will allow you to save your progress, access it from any device, and personalize your experience.

Below, we explain how to **register** and **log in** step-by-step.

---

## 📝 Create an Account

To create your account, go to 👉 [habituo.es/register](https://habituo.es/register)

On this page, you will find a simple form with the following fields:

- **Full Name:** enter your first and last name as you want them to appear on your profile.  
- **Email Address:** make sure to enter a valid email, as you will use it to log in or recover your password if you forget it.  
- **Password:** create a secure password that only you know.  
- **Repeat Password:** confirm that both passwords match.  

Once you complete the fields, click the **"Create Account"** button.

> 💡 Tip: choose a password that combines letters, numbers, and symbols for greater security.

---

### 🔐 Register with Google

If you prefer to do it faster, you can **register with your Google account**.  
Just click the **"Register with Google"** button, select your account, and Habituo will take care of the rest. It's that easy!

---

### 🚀 Already have an account?

If you are already registered, you can click the **"Log In"** link below the form to directly access your account.

---

## 🔑 Log In

To enter your account, go to 👉 [habituo.es/login](https://habituo.es/login)

You have two ways to access:

1. **With your email and password:**  
   - Enter your email address.  
   - Enter your password.  
   - Click **"Log In"**.

2. **With your Google account:**  
   - Click **"Log In with Google"**.  
   - Select your account, and you will automatically access your dashboard.

---

### 🔁 Recover Password

Forgot your password? Don't worry.  
On the log-in page, select the option **"Forgot your password?"** (or "¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?").  

Then:
1. Enter the **email address** associated with your account.  
2. You will receive a **link in your email** to reset your password.  
3. Follow the instructions in the email, and you can create a new password in minutes.

> 📧 If you don't see the email, check your *spam* or *promotions* folder.

---

## 🎉 Done!

Once you have created your account or logged in, you will be able to access your **habit dashboard**, create your first routines, and begin your journey towards a more balanced and productive life with **Habituo**.
